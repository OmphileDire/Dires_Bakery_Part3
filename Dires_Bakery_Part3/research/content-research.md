# Content Research and Sourcing - Dire's Bakery

## Organisation research
The organisation is a hypothetical neighbourhood bakery based on the approved project proposal. The proposal describes the business as offering fresh bread, pastries and custom celebration cakes. It also identifies local families, students and professionals as the main audience.

## Website content decisions
### Home page
The homepage introduces the bakery, explains what it sells and gives direct links to products and custom cake enquiries.

### About page
The page explains the bakery's fictional origin, mission and vision. This follows the proposal's story-driven approach.

### Products page
The initial products are fresh bread, butter croissants, vanilla cupcakes and custom celebration cakes. The prices are presented as example student-project prices and can be changed later.

### Enquiry page
The page is focused on custom cake enquiries. The fields ask for information that would be useful for an initial enquiry, such as name, email, event date and order details.

### Contact page
The proposal requires contact information and a map. Because the organisation is hypothetical and no verified physical address was provided, the Part 1 site uses a map placeholder rather than inventing a real business location.

## Technical research
1. W3C explains that semantic elements such as `header`, `nav`, `main` and `footer` identify important regions of a web page and can help assistive technologies navigate the page.
2. MDN explains that HTML form validation can use attributes such as `required`, `type`, `minlength`, `maxlength`, `min`, `max`, `step` and `pattern`.
3. Unsplash states that its images can generally be downloaded and used for personal and commercial purposes under the Unsplash License. For this Part 1 project I used original SVG illustrations instead, so the image assets in this repository do not depend on an external image licence.
4. Google Maps documentation explains that interactive maps can be embedded in web pages. A real map will be considered in the later implementation once a confirmed location is available.
