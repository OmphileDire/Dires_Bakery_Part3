About us

Our story

A small bakery built around fresh baking and community.

How it started

Dire's Bakery is a small hypothetical neighbourhood bakery founded by Omphile Dire. It began as a home-based baking project based on a personal interest in baking and entrepreneurship.

The bakery's focus is on fresh bread, pastries and custom celebration cakes. The aim is to provide customers with good quality baked goods while keeping the experience friendly and personal.

Mission

To deliver freshly baked, high-quality products with a personal and community-focused service.

Vision

To become a well-loved local bakery known for its products and the personal story behind the business.
