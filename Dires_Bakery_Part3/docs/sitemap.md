```text

                          |
          +---------------+---------------+
          |               |               |
       ABOUT          PRODUCTS         ENQUIRY
   (about.html)    (products.html)   (enquiry.html)
                                          |
                                       CONTACT
                                   (contact.html)

Global navigation appears on every page:
Home | About Us | Products | Enquiry | Contact
```

## Page purpose
- **Home:** introduction, call-to-action and product highlights.
- **About Us:** history, mission and vision.
- **Products:** current example product list.
- **Enquiry:** custom cake enquiry form.
- **Contact:** contact details, opening hours and map area.
