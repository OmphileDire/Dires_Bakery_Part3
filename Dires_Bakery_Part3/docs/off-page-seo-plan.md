# Off-page SEO plan

Off-page SEO requires activity outside the website. These are proposed actions; they are not claimed as completed backlinks or social campaigns.

1. Create consistent bakery business profiles on suitable local directories and social platforms after the business details are confirmed.
2. Share product photographs, opening-hour updates and links to the website on the bakery's social media pages.
3. Ask genuine local partners, community organisations or event planners to link to the bakery website when they mention the business.
4. Keep the business name, address and contact details consistent across public listings.
5. Review referral traffic and search performance after the website has been deployed and connected to analytics/search tools.

For this student project, the bakery is hypothetical. Do not create fake reviews, fake business listings or misleading backlinks. These steps are a plan for a real business, not actions completed for the assignment.
