## 1. Organisation Overview
Mokoena Home Cleaning is a hypothetical small cleaning service operating in Johannesburg. The business provides general home cleaning, deep cleaning and move-in or move-out cleaning. It is aimed at households and busy professionals who need a reliable cleaning service.

The mission is to provide simple, dependable and friendly cleaning services. The vision is to become a trusted local cleaning business with a strong reputation for good service.

## 2. Website Goals and Objectives
The website would give the business a professional online presence and make it easier for customers to understand the services and request a quote.

Objectives:
- Increase local awareness.
- Explain the different cleaning services.
- Generate quote requests.
- Make contact information easy to find.

Possible KPIs are monthly quote enquiries, page visits and the number of users who reach the enquiry page.

## 3. Current Website Analysis
The business is hypothetical and currently has no website. Without a website, customers have less information available before contacting the business. A website could solve this by showing services, basic pricing information, service areas and contact details.

## 4. Proposed Website Features and Functionality
The website would include:
- Home page with a short introduction and call to action.
- About page explaining the business.
- Services page with cleaning packages.
- Enquiry page for quote requests.
- Contact page with contact details and a map.
- Simple enquiry form with client-side validation.

## 5. Design and User Experience
The design would use clean neutral colours with a blue accent. Headings would use a simple sans-serif font and body text would be easy to read. The layout would use clear service cards and a consistent navigation menu.

## 6. Technical Requirements
The site would use HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The project would be developed in Visual Studio Code and stored in GitHub. A domain and shared hosting provider would be required for a live version.

## 7. Timeline and Milestones
- Week 1: Planning and research.
- Week 2: Sitemap and HTML structure.
- Week 3: Content and navigation.
- Week 4: Testing and documentation.
- Later parts: CSS, responsiveness, JavaScript and SEO.
